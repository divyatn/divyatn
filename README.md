# Hi, I'm Divya 👋

**Analytical Scientist | Bioinformatics | RNA-seq & Multi-omics**

I am an analytical scientist with 8.5 years of experience in analytical R&D, chromatography, and mass spectrometry. I am expanding my expertise into computational biology through structured, reproducible analysis of public omics datasets.

## Areas of Interest

- RNA-seq differential gene expression analysis
- Pathway and functional enrichment analysis
- Single-cell RNA-seq and multi-omics
- Proteomics, LC-MS/MS, and biomarker discovery
- Reproducible scientific workflows

## Technical Skills

**Analytical science:** LC-MS, GC-MS, LC-MS/MS, GC-MS/MS, IC, method development and validation

**Bioinformatics:** Galaxy, R, Bioconductor, DESeq2, clusterProfiler

**Learning and workflow development:** Linux, Python, Git, GitHub

## Selected Projects

- **RNA-seq analysis:** Developing a reproducible workflow for differential expression and downstream functional interpretation using public gene-expression data.

- **Future direction:** Integrating transcriptomics and proteomics to investigate biological pathways and potential biomarkers.

*Project repositories and validated results will be added as they are documented and cleared for sharing.*

## Research Philosophy
